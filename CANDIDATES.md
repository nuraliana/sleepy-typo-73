# Candidates — Funniest Autocorrect Fail

Scored 1–5 on each criterion (total /25):

- **C1 Clarity of the slip** — the wrong word is clearly one autocorrect step
  from the right word (one letter, a plausible keyboard/autocorrect swap).
- **C2 Surprise of the wrong word** — how unexpected the swapped word is.
- **C3 Lands on first read** — instant laugh, no explanation needed.
- **C4 Consequence / escalation** — the fail causes something funny to happen.
- **C5 Freshness vs the field** — not a saturated angle among the 155 entries.

  escalates from one text to a lasting, humiliating reputation at work.
- **C5 5:** absent from all 155 submission titles in the field analysis.
